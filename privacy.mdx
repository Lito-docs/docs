# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** February 2026

## Overview

Lito ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our documentation platform.

## Information We Collect

### Usage Data

When you visit our documentation site, we may collect anonymous usage data including:

- Pages visited and time spent
- Browser type and version
- Device type and screen resolution
- Referring website

### Analytics

If analytics are enabled, we use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how our documentation is used. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify individual users.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Improve documentation content and structure
- Fix broken links and navigation issues
- Understand which topics need more coverage
- Optimize site performance

## Data Storage

- We do not store personal information
- Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized
- No cookies are used for tracking purposes

## Third-Party Services

Our documentation may use the following third-party services:

- **Search (Pagefind)** — Runs entirely client-side, no data sent to external servers
- **Fonts (Google Fonts)** — Subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access any data we hold about you
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt out of analytics tracking

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Lito-docs/cli).
