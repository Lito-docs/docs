## Submit your site

Built something with Lito? We want to showcase it! Here's how to get listed:

1. **Open a PR** — Add your site to [this page](https://github.com/Lito-docs/docs/edit/main/community/showcase.mdx) with a title, short description, and link.
2. **Drop a message on Discord** — Share your site in the `#showcase` channel and we'll add it.

All sites are welcome — developer tools, open-source projects, company docs, personal projects, and more.
